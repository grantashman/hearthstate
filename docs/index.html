The point is not more admin

Less remembering. More being together.

A household already has enough invisible work. Hearthstate makes that work legible without making it the centre of the room.

  • Capture without commitmentPut a thought somewhere trusted before deciding what it needs to become.
  • Ownership without naggingMake the next action clear and shared instead of keeping a private list of who owes what.
  • Progress without performanceSee what needs attention, then get back to the life underneath it.

A lighter loop for the work between people.

2
